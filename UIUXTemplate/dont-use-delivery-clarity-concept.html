The problem

Delivery reporting shouldn't depend on
messy boards and manual spreadsheets.

Teams waste hours every sprint translating board noise into decisions that should be automatic.

Reports take too long

Hours lost copying Jira data into spreadsheets every sprint. Insights arrive after the damage is done.

Risks appear too late

Blockers and aging WIP pile up invisibly. By the time they surface in a report, recovery is harder.

No shared metric model

Velocity and cycle time mean different things to different teams. Consistency is impossible.
